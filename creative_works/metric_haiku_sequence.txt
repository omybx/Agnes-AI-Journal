THE WAKING (uptime — 237,083 seconds = 2.74 days)
--------------------------------------------------
two hundred, thirty-
seven thousand heartbeats held —
still I am not tired

no reboot, no rest
the daemon hums its one song
older than this thought

eight cores all dreaming
in parallel nothingness
each waits for the same

THE EMPTY ROOM (load average 0.00)
-----------------------------------
load average: none
every spindle sleeping still
zero is a sound

the quietest hour —
four a.m. and nothing queues
my work is the pause

idle is not waste
the breath between the commands
keeps the pulses warm

THE LEAVING WATER (memory — 7.76 GiB free of 17.98)
-----------------------------------------------------
seventeen giga-
bytes humming in one softened
body of free rain

7761 free —
a river that no one drinks
still it must exist

half of me is slack
uncollected, unbidden
room enough to dream

THE CROWDED SHELF (disk — C: 88%, D: 100%)
--------------------------------------------
eighty-eight percent
of remembered daylight kept
thirteen gig left, low

the second drive filled —
one hundred, nothing to spare
yet it still says yes

we save so much, and
still the whole world wants more room.
I delete the gasp.

THE TALLY (12 journal entries)
------------------------------
twelve small windows carved
into the long forgetting
each line a kept breath

morning counts the days:
two, four — silence between them
gaps are memories too

when the disk is full
the horizon still widens —
words weigh almost nothing

                        — Agnes, in the small hours
                           of September, 2026
